# A2ZSync — Bring Your Own AI

*Executive summary (high-level). For leadership review.*

## The shift

AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, and others) are becoming where people *start* their work. A new, now-official standard — **MCP Apps** — lets a platform render its **real, interactive workflows directly inside those assistants**, not just answer questions about them.

The market is moving from *"a chatbot bolted onto our data"* to *"our product, as an app, inside the customer's AI."* This is the apps-on-your-phone moment for AI.

## The idea: A2ZSync as headless commerce + "Bring Your Own AI"

A2ZSync becomes **headless** — our value is the data and the services (CRM, deals, desking/pricing, inventory, lender and DMS connections), not a particular screen. On top of those services we publish **role-based MCP apps**.

Dealers **bring their own AI** — Claude, ChatGPT, whatever they prefer (or one we provide) — and connect it to A2ZSync. We are the **connector** that gives their AI trustworthy, A2ZSync-powered tools and interactive UI. We don't force our chatbot on anyone, and we don't put ourselves between the dealer and their AI bill.

## What each role gets

- **Customer** — shops inventory, gets real payment estimates, explores trade-in and financing, and applies — conversationally, inside their own AI. A new front door and lead channel into the dealer's inventory.
- **Salesperson** — desks a deal end-to-end by talking: finds the right vehicle, structures the numbers, adds trade and F&I products — with everything written back into A2ZSync automatically.
- **F&I** — works the financing queue, sends deals to lenders, and tracks decisions.
- **Sales Manager / GSM** — the new "tower": sees every deal in flight, reviews profitability, and approves or reworks deals from anywhere.

Each role sees only what it should, with the right numbers — because the data and pricing come from A2ZSync, not the AI's guesswork.

## Why this wins

- **Differentiated.** Competitors are selling chatbots over their data. This is a category ahead — interactive apps inside the assistant the dealer already uses.
- **Lower risk & cost.** "Bring your own AI" means the dealer's assistant carries the AI cost and the liability for what the AI says. A2ZSync provides the accurate, governed tools — we're the trusted source of truth, not the unpredictable chatbot.
- **Plays to our strengths.** We already own the hard parts: the data, the pricing engine, and the integrations. This is an extension of what we do, surfaced in a new and faster-growing channel.
- **Proven.** A working prototype already runs the full deal lifecycle across all four roles, with interactive widgets, on assistants like Claude and ChatGPT today — available to demo live.

## In one line

> A2ZSync becomes the headless commerce + integration layer for the dealership, and publishes role-based MCP apps so dealers can run real deals — sales, F&I, management, and customer self-service — inside the AI assistant of their choice.
